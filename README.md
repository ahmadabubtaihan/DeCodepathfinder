# 🧭 DECODE PATH FINDER

**DECODE PATH FINDER** is a comprehensive, bilingual (Arabic & English) web platform designed for Information Technology and Engineering students in Palestine (specifically the Gaza Strip) to help them choose their optimal career paths, find curated educational resources, explore required computer hardware specifications, and discover local field training and job opportunities.

---

## 📌 Overview | نظرة عامة

Due to the rapid evolution of Information Technology fields, students often struggle to determine which specialization fits their skills and career goals, what hardware specifications they need, and where to find reliable courses or practical training. **DECODE PATH FINDER** consolidates all of these resources into one accessible, user-friendly platform.

---

## ✨ Key Features | المميزات الرئيسية

- **Career Tracks & Domains (المسارات والتخصصات التقنية):** Detailed roadmaps and guides covering Web Development (Full Stack), Mobile App Development (Android/iOS), CMS, UI/UX, Graphic Design, Game Programming, and IT Basics.
- **Courses Directory (دليل الدورات التدريبية):** Browse, enroll in, and review both free and paid courses with progress tracking.
- **Hardware Recommendations (مواصفات الأجهزة المقترحة):** Tailored PC/Laptop hardware specifications for each IT specialization along with recommended local stores for purchasing and maintenance.
- **Training & Job Opportunities (فرص التدريب الميداني والوظائف):** Explore and apply for local internships, workspaces, and tech jobs in Palestine.
- **Smart Search Engine (محرك بحث ذكي):** Quickly search across courses, tools, hardware specs, and training programs with category filtering.
- **Bilingual Support (دعم ثنائي اللغة):** Seamless switching between **Arabic** and **English**.
- **Role-Based Access & Support (صلاحيات متعددة ودعم فني):** Dedicated dashboards and workflows for **Admins**, **Students**, **Visitors**, and **Technical Support**.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture | التقنيات المستخدمة

- **Architecture:** MVC (Model-View-Controller)
- **Methodology:** Agile — Extreme Programming (XP)
- **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Bootstrap
- **Backend:** PHP, Laravel Framework
- **Database:** MySQL
- **Design & Prototyping:** Adobe XD, Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator

---

## 👥 User Roles | أدوار المستخدمين في النظام

1. **Admin:** Full control over platform content (courses, domains, tools, jobs, training), user accounts, reviews, and system monitoring.
2. **Student:** Register/Login (Email or Google), enroll in free/paid courses, track learning progress, rate/comment on courses, and contact technical support.
3. **Visitor:** Browse public domains, hardware specifications, job listings, and training opportunities without requiring an account.
4. **Technical Supporter:** Monitor system status, respond to user inquiries, troubleshoot technical issues, and escalate complex cases to the Admin.
